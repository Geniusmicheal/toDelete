Quantrium Impact is the social impact and development foundation of Quantrium Holdings Limited, established to advance shared prosperity through enterprise development and job creation. The Foundation is sector-agnostic, enabling it to support small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs) across diverse industries based on impact potential rather than sector classification.
